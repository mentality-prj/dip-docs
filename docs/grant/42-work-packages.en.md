## Purpose
Сформувати структуру Work Packages для реалізації R&D програми Dzvin.co + DIP.

## WP Overview
- WP1 Project Management and Governance
- WP2 Data Foundation and Compliance
- WP3 Decision Intelligence Core (DIP)
- WP4 Explainability, Safety, and Validation
- WP5 Dzvin.co Integration and Pilots
- WP6 Impact, Dissemination, and Exploitation

## WP1: Management and Governance
- Objectives: координація, quality gates, risk governance.
- Deliverables: management plan, periodic reports, risk updates.

## WP2: Data Foundation and Compliance
- Objectives: dataset readiness, quality, GDPR/ethics controls.
- Deliverables: dataset specs, quality dashboards, compliance dossier.

## WP3: Decision Intelligence Core
- Objectives: rule/ML/policy engine hardening, traceability.
- Deliverables: architecture releases, decision contract versions.

## WP4: Explainability and Validation
- Objectives: explainability framework, offline/online evaluation.
- Deliverables: evaluation reports, bias/safety audits, reproducibility pack.

## WP5: Dzvin.co Pilots
- Objectives: product integration, controlled rollout, user validation.
- Deliverables: pilot outcomes, adoption metrics, implementation learnings.

## WP6: Impact and Exploitation
- Objectives: dissemination, commercialization preparation, partner uptake.
- Deliverables: impact reports, exploitation roadmap, dissemination outputs.

## Milestones (Draft)
- M1: Baseline and protocol freeze
- M2: Hybrid engine v1 validated offline
- M3: Controlled online evaluation complete
- M4: Pilot evidence package complete
- M5: Grant final impact dossier

## Dependencies
- WP2 gates WP3 and WP4.
- WP3 outputs feed WP5 rollout.
- WP4 evidence informs WP6 impact narrative.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Кожен WP має measurable deliverables.
- Milestones прив'язані до evaluation gates.
- Всі критичні міжпакетні залежності явні.

## Purpose
Сформувати комерційну стратегію для Dzvin.co + DIP після R&D фази з акцентом на sustainability та масштабування.

## Commercial Model
- Dzvin.co: B2B/B2B2C wellbeing solution for organizations.
- DIP: platform licensing/integration model for decision intelligence use-cases.
- Combined strategy: application revenue + platform leverage.

## Target Segments
- Mid-size and enterprise employers.
- HR/People Operations teams.
- Partner ecosystems (integrators, consultancies, health programs).
- Future cross-domain adopters for DIP modules.

## Value Propositions
- Faster and more explainable risk-aware decisions.
- Lower adoption friction via actionable outputs.
- Auditability for governance-focused buyers.

## Go-to-Market Phases
1. Validation pilots and reference customers.
2. Repeatable deployment package.
3. Regional scaling with partner channels.
4. Platform extension into adjacent domains.

## Pricing and Packaging (Draft)
- Dzvin.co: tiered subscription by active population and modules.
- DIP: usage/license components by decision volume and capabilities.
- Professional services: onboarding and governance setup.

## Commercial Risks
- Long enterprise sales cycles.
- Procurement and compliance barriers.
- Need for localized regulatory adaptation.

## Mitigation
- Build evidence-led sales assets from R&D outcomes.
- Standardized compliance documentation package.
- Partner-led market entry in regulated sectors.

## Success Metrics
- Pilot-to-paid conversion rate.
- Net revenue retention.
- Time-to-value after onboarding.
- Platform attachment rate (DIP-enabled modules).

## Acceptance Criteria
- Є чіткий шлях від R&D результатів до revenue streams.
- GTM гіпотези підкріплені pilot evidence.
- Commercial KPI framework узгоджений з impact KPI.

## Short pitch draft

QDIP addresses a recurring engineering problem: every operational decision domain tends to rebuild its own data semantics, constraints, uncertainty logic, optimization integration and audit trail. QDIP provides a reusable formal runtime around a Canonical Decision IR. Our claim is not that we invented a better solver; it is that the same frozen runtime can preserve decision quality versus specialized implementations while cutting the engineering effort required to deploy a new decision domain. We are testing that claim through preregistered benchmarks in readiness recovery, procurement and a blind third domain. Startup EDGE would fund a bounded evidence and productization milestone, turning the current technical prototype into a grant- and customer-verifiable deep-tech asset.

## Answer bank

### What problem do you solve?
Repeated bespoke implementation of operational decision systems makes deployment slow, expensive and difficult to audit. QDIP aims to reuse formal decision semantics across domains.

### What is technically innovative?
The Canonical Decision IR/runtime boundary and the empirical cross-domain preservation test. The innovation is measured against specialized implementations rather than asserted from architecture alone.

### Why is this not ordinary software integration?
The core uncertainty is whether heterogeneous decision domains can be represented through a frozen semantic/runtime interface without decision-quality degradation or hidden bespoke optimization. That is a research and systems problem tested through falsifiable benchmarks.

### What advantage are you testing?
Per domain, reference-normalized decision loss must be non-inferior within a preregistered margin and domain-marginal engineering effort must be at most 50% of the specialized implementation.

### What evidence exists now?
Executable platform, formal decision/intake/replay/audit infrastructure, extensive automated testing, Canonical Evidence Pack and preregistered Test A protocol. Primary Test A/B/C results are not claimed until executed.

### Who pays?
The commercial hypothesis targets organisations with recurring structured decisions where bespoke integration and governance are material costs. This must be validated through pilots/LOIs and paid deployments.

### Why Ukraine?
QDIP can create exportable Ukrainian deep-tech software/IP and reduce the cost of deploying auditable decision-support applications in resource-constrained and resilience-sensitive operations.

### What will the grant change?
It funds a bounded evidence/product milestone: primary benchmark execution, cross-domain validation where budget permits, deployment hardening and external validation readiness.

### What happens if the research gate fails?
The result is preserved. The thesis is narrowed or rejected rather than rescued through post-hoc tuning. This is part of the project's technical credibility.

## One-sentence product

QDIP is a reusable decision runtime that converts structured operational problems into constraint-valid, uncertainty-aware, economically evaluable and auditable recommendations without rebuilding a bespoke decision stack for every domain.

## Problem

Organisations repeatedly rebuild decision logic for procurement, resource allocation, readiness, logistics and similar operations. BI systems describe what happened; optimization libraries provide algorithms; bespoke projects connect domain semantics, constraints, uncertainty, solver logic, deployment and auditability anew for each use case. This creates high engineering cost, slow validation and fragmented decision governance.

## Innovation

QDIP introduces a Canonical Decision IR and frozen runtime boundary intended to preserve decision semantics across domains. The empirical product thesis is deliberately falsifiable:

```text
NR_QDIP^(d) <= NR_specialized^(d) + epsilon_d
E_domain,QDIP^(d) <= 0.5 * E_domain,specialized^(d)
```

The novelty claim is not a new solver. It is reusable formal decision semantics/runtime plus measurable cross-domain preservation of decision quality and lower implementation effort.

## Why Deep Tech

The technical risk is substantive: the project must determine whether a frozen intermediate representation, uncertainty/utility semantics, solver boundary and extension model can generalize across heterogeneous operational domains without hiding bespoke logic in plugins. The answer requires preregistered benchmarks, reference-normalized regret/loss, cross-domain falsification and reproducible evidence — not ordinary CRUD/product integration.

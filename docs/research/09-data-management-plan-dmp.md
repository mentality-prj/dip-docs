# Data Management Plan (DMP)

## Мета
Визначити життєвий цикл даних за FAIR-принципами і вимогами європейських програм.

## FAIR Mapping
- Findable: data catalog + stable identifiers.
- Accessible: role-based controlled access.
- Interoperable: documented schemas and vocabularies.
- Reusable: metadata quality + licensing constraints.

## Data Lifecycle
1. Collection
2. Validation and quality control
3. Feature and label derivation
4. Storage and protection
5. Controlled sharing/export
6. Retention and deletion/anonymization

## Governance
- Data owner per dataset.
- Access approval workflow.
- Audit logging for sensitive operations.

## Security and Privacy
- Pseudonymization for research exports.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- Cross-border transfer safeguards.

## Reuse Policy
- Internal reuse with purpose limitation.
- External sharing only in aggregated/de-identified form and under legal basis.

## DMP Review Cadence
- Quarterly review or after major architecture/regulatory changes.

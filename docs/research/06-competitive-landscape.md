# Competitive Landscape

## Мета
Порівняти:
- DIP з Decision Intelligence / MLOps платформами.
- Dzvin.co з mental wellbeing продуктами.

## Comparison Axes
- Explainability depth
- Governance and policy controls
- Reproducibility readiness
- Domain adaptation cost
- B2B operational actionability

## DIP vs Platform Alternatives (Framework)
| Axis | DIP | Typical MLOps Platform |
|------|-----|-------------------------|
| Hybrid rule+ML governance | Native target architecture | Often external/custom |
| Decision trace as contract | Core requirement | Usually optional |
| Domain adapter pattern | Explicit | Varies |
| Mental wellbeing specialization | Via Dzvin.co integration | Usually absent |

## Dzvin.co vs Wellbeing Alternatives (Framework)
| Axis | Dzvin.co | Typical wellbeing product |
|------|----------|---------------------------|
| B2B action recommendations | Integrated target | Often dashboard-first |
| Explainable risk outputs | Explicit target | Often limited |
| R&D traceability integration | Platform-linked | Rare |

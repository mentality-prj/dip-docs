# Consortium Handbook

## Мета
Описати ролі та відповідальності партнерів для грантового виконання.

## Partner Types
- Platform engineering partner
- Product and pilot partner
- Research/academic partner
- Clinical advisory partner
- Compliance/legal support partner

## Role Model
- Coordinator: governance, reporting, risk steering.
- Technical lead: architecture and delivery quality.
- Scientific lead: methodology and publication quality.
- Pilot lead: deployment and adoption outcomes.

## Operating Model
- Decision cadence (weekly ops, monthly steering).
- Artifact ownership and review flow.
- Conflict resolution and change control.

## Mandatory Annexes
- RACI matrix
- Communication protocol
- Escalation matrix

# 16 — Ukrainian and EU Impact

Status: active reusable grant module

## Ukraine

QDIP can contribute to Ukrainian innovation capacity by reducing the engineering cost of deploying auditable decision-support software across resource-constrained operational environments. Relevant application families may include resilience/readiness, logistics, procurement, infrastructure, agriculture and other operational allocation problems.

The strongest Ukraine-specific impact case is not abstract AI research. It is evidence that a Ukrainian-origin technology can shorten the path from operational problem to validated decision application while preserving transparent constraints, human authority and measurable economic/operational outcomes.

## European Union

The EU-facing impact hypothesis is:

- more reusable digital decision infrastructure for SMEs and operational organisations;
- lower integration burden across heterogeneous decision domains;
- auditable and reproducible AI/optimization-assisted decisions;
- support for resilient supply/resource allocation and digital transformation;
- reduced dependence on opaque domain-specific stacks where QDIP proves equivalent quality with lower implementation effort.

## Measurable impact indicators

Use only indicators supported by the funded scope, for example:

- domain integration time/engineering effort;
- decision-quality non-inferiority;
- realized cost/loss/risk reduction in a pilot;
- number of validated decision domains;
- external pilot/LOI;
- deployment/audit reproducibility;
- number of organisations testing or adopting the runtime.

## Defence/dual-use rule

Defence framing belongs only in a call-specific overlay where directly relevant. The generic QDIP evidence pack remains domain-neutral and human-authority preserving.

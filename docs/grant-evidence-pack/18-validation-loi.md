# 18 — Validation Partner / LOI Package

Status: reusable template

An external validator is desirable evidence, not a mandatory consortium partner unless the target call explicitly requires a consortium.

## Target validator

Prefer an organisation with a real recurring decision in one of:

- readiness/resource recovery;
- procurement;
- logistics/inventory/fleet/workforce;
- another domain that can expose decision inputs, constraints and measurable outcome without requiring product-control concessions.

## Minimum useful LOI content

A non-binding LOI should confirm:

1. the organisation has the stated operational decision problem;
2. the problem has measurable cost/risk/service/readiness consequences;
3. it is willing to review or pilot a bounded QDIP application if technical/legal conditions are met;
4. it can provide representative data or a safe synthetic/derived equivalent where possible;
5. it is willing to provide structured validation feedback;
6. the letter creates no procurement, exclusivity, IP transfer or consortium obligation unless separately agreed.

## LOI template

```text
To whom it may concern,

[Organisation] confirms its interest in evaluating QDIP, a reusable decision runtime for structured operational decisions under uncertainty, in the context of [decision problem].

The relevant operational problem concerns [brief description], where outcomes can be evaluated using [cost/risk/service/readiness metric]. Subject to appropriate confidentiality, data-protection and technical arrangements, we are willing to discuss a bounded validation/pilot, provide representative requirements and review the resulting decision workflow and evidence.

This letter is non-binding and does not constitute a purchase commitment, consortium agreement, exclusivity arrangement, IP assignment or guarantee of future deployment.

Name / role / organisation / date / signature
```

## Validation evidence to collect

- decision-owner role;
- current baseline process;
- data availability;
- constraints and objective;
- decision cadence;
- economic/operational KPI;
- acceptance criteria;
- post-pilot feedback.

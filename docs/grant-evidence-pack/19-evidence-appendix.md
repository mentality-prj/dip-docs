## Repository evidence classes

Applications may additionally reference versioned code/tests/manifests from the QDIP repository for:

- Core/runtime behavior;
- automated tests and CI;
- decision-intake and Canonical IR implementation;
- replay/audit/economic-value tooling;
- benchmark preregistrations and immutable hashes;
- domain adapters and validation artifacts.

## Evidence maturity labels

Use one of:

- `ESTABLISHED` — directly supported by archived evidence;
- `PREREGISTERED` — protocol frozen, result not yet known;
- `DEVELOPMENT` — internal exploratory evidence only;
- `HYPOTHESIS` — intended effect/business claim not yet validated;
- `TBD` — missing factual field required before submission.

No `DEVELOPMENT`, `HYPOTHESIS` or `TBD` item may be rewritten as an established grant claim merely for narrative strength.
